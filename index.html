Why Visit an Orthodontist in Dayton TN?

Finding a trusted Orthodontist in Dayton TN can help you address dental alignment concerns before they become more complicated. Orthodontists specialise in diagnosing and treating problems involving teeth positioning, jaw alignment, and bite development.

Crooked teeth and bite issues are not just cosmetic concerns. They can make brushing and flossing difficult, increase the risk of plaque buildup, and contribute to uneven tooth wear. An orthodontic assessment helps identify these problems and determine the most suitable treatment options.

Professional orthodontic treatment can help with:

  • Crooked or crowded teeth
  • Gaps and spacing between teeth
  • Overbites and underbites
  • Crossbites and open bites
  • Misaligned jaws
  • Difficulty chewing properly
  • Uneven tooth positioning

Early evaluation can also help parents understand whether their children may benefit from orthodontic treatment as their permanent teeth develop.

Orthodontic Solutions for Children, Teens, and Adults

Orthodontic care is beneficial at different stages of life. Children may need early orthodontic monitoring to identify developing bite problems, while teenagers often benefit from braces to correct permanent teeth alignment. Adults can also achieve straighter teeth through modern orthodontic solutions.

Webb Family Orthodontics offers several treatment options, including:

Traditional Metal Braces

Metal braces remain a popular solution for correcting crooked teeth, overcrowding, and complex bite problems. They use brackets and wires to apply gentle pressure, gradually moving teeth into their desired positions.

Clear and Ceramic Braces

Clear and ceramic braces provide an alternative for patients who prefer a less noticeable orthodontic appliance. These braces work similarly to traditional braces while offering a more discreet appearance.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, removable clear aligners to gradually straighten teeth. They are a convenient option for eligible patients who want flexibility when eating, brushing, and flossing.

Early Orthodontic Treatment

Children can benefit from early orthodontic evaluations because their teeth and jaws are still developing. Monitoring growth allows an orthodontist to identify potential alignment concerns and recommend treatment at the appropriate time.

Maintaining Your Smile During Orthodontic Treatment

Good oral hygiene is essential while wearing braces or clear aligners. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups help protect your teeth and gums throughout the treatment process.

Patients with traditional braces should avoid hard, sticky, and chewy foods that may damage brackets or wires. Those using clear aligners should follow their orthodontist's instructions regarding daily wear and cleaning.

Following professional recommendations and attending scheduled appointments can help keep treatment progressing as planned.
